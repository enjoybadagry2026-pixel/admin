6. Rides, driver assignment, fares, cancellations & completion

Requesting a ride

When you request a ride you choose a pickup point and a destination. The app sends your request to available drivers, and a driver may accept it. To help the trip go smoothly, your driver receives your name and phone number when they accept your ride, and you see information about your driver in the app.

Fares

Before you confirm a booking, the app shows a fare estimate with a breakdown — base fare, distance charge, booking fee and service fee. The vehicle types offered are a standard car, an SUV and a van, with different capacities and base fares. The final fare shown when your trip is priced on the platform is the amount that applies; estimates can differ from the final figure if the actual trip differs from what was estimated.

Cancellations

You may cancel a ride request, and the app asks for a reason. Please cancel as early as you can once you know you no longer need the trip, out of respect for the driver's time. Any cancellation terms or charges that apply will be shown to you in the app before or when you cancel — these Terms do not create any cancellation charge that is not shown in the app.

Completion, ratings & reports

A ride is completed when the trip ends on the platform. You can rate your driver afterwards. If something goes wrong, you can report a driver in the app — for example for rude behaviour, unsafe driving, harassment, a wrong route or detour, vehicle condition, or another reason. Reports are reviewed by our team.

Ride conversations with your driver, trip records and ratings are stored so the service works reliably and so issues can be investigated — see our Privacy Policy.

8. Wallet balances, deposits, payments & transaction records

The wallet

Enjoy Badagry includes a naira (NGN) wallet used to pay for rides and food orders in the app. Your wallet balance and transaction history are visible in the app.

Adding money

You add money to your wallet by bank transfer. The app issues you a virtual bank account for the transfer, and payments are processed by Flutterwave. After your bank transfer is received and verified, the funds are credited to your wallet. You do not enter card or bank card numbers into the Enjoy Badagry app.

Paying with the wallet

When you pay for a ride or food order with your wallet, the amount is deducted from your balance and a record is created in your transaction history, showing the transaction type, amount, references and status.

Payment disputes & corrections

If you believe a wallet transaction is wrong — for example a deposit that was not credited or a charge you do not recognise — contact us through the in-app Help & Support chat with the transaction details. We will review the platform's transaction records and, where a correction is due, adjust your wallet accordingly. Please report issues as soon as you notice them.

9. Food orders, pricing, fulfilment, cancellations & refunds

Placing an order

You can browse menus in the app — including ingredients, serving sizes and spicy levels where provided — customise your order, and have it delivered to an address you choose. You provide the recipient name, phone number, delivery address and any delivery instructions, which are used to deliver the order.

Pricing

Before you place an order, the app shows a full price breakdown: the food total, any extras you add, the delivery fee, packaging fee, platform fee, service fee, any small order fee, any coupon discount, and the grand total. Orders are paid from your wallet.

Fulfilment & tracking

After you order, the restaurant confirms and prepares it, and a rider delivers it. You can follow the order's status and, while it is out for delivery, track the rider's progress in the app.

Cancellations & refunds

You can ask to cancel an order, but whether it can still be cancelled depends on its current status — once preparation or delivery has begun, a cancellation may no longer be possible. If an order is cancelled or a refund is issued, the refund is returned to your wallet and appears in your transaction history. These Terms do not promise any refund beyond what the app and our support team make available for your specific order.
