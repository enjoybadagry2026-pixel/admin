Frequently asked questions

What do I need to apply?

Provide your contact details, licence status, driving experience, vehicle information, operating area and availability. No document uploads are requested here.

Does applying guarantee approval?

No. Applications are reviewed and approval is not guaranteed. Approved applicants will be contacted.

Does this create a driver account?

No. Driver account/DRT setup and any further verification are separate from this application.
