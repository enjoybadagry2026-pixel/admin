Before you set off

A simple trip checklist

Keep the essentials in mind and check the latest information before you travel.

  • Confirm opening hours, entrance fees and directions.
  • Check travel and return arrangements, and allow extra time.
  • Confirm food or accommodation details directly where needed.
  • Bring what you need for the weather and your planned activities.
  • Keep your phone charged and your booking references handy.
