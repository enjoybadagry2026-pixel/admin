Delete Your Account

This permanently removes your account and personal data from Enjoy Badagry. This cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number and photo
  • Saved delivery addresses, favorites and likes
  • Notifications, support conversations and chat messages
  • Your wallet account — you need a zero balance first
  • Your email sign-in, so you can’t log in with it again

What we keep (anonymous)

  • Completed rides, orders and hotel bookings, with your name and contact details removed
  • Your transaction history, with payment details removed
  • Your reviews and comments, shown as “Deleted User”

Deleting is permanent. There is no way to restore your account, purchases or profile afterwards. Active rides or orders and a remaining wallet balance must be settled first.
